Research Interests

My research focuses on manifold learning and its applications. High dimensional data commonly has low dimensional structure. A typical model of underlying structure is an unknown manifold. Manifold learning applies techniques in differential geometry to explore the underlying data structure. Specifically, my research interests include the following directions.

  1. Dimension reduction
    I establish the mathematical foundation for nonlinear dimension reduction algorithms. Some nonlinear dimension reduction algorithms that I study include Locally Linear Embedding and Diffusion Maps (kernel graph Laplacian).
  2. Nonparametric statistics on manifold
    I apply techniques in manifold learning to study problems in nonparametric statistics, in particular Gaussian process regression.
  3. Developing new manifold learning methods with theoretical foundation.
